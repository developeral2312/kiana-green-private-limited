ENGINEERING STANDARDS

5-Point Technical & Financial Evaluation

We don't just sell components. We design unified systems where every piece of hardware is verified for compatibility and maximum energy yield.

Technical Parameters
1. Load Profiling & Sizing Crucial

Analyzing hour-by-hour electrical consumption to prevent under/over sizing.

2. Shadow & Site Analysis PVsyst/Sketchup

3D modeling to determine optimal tilt, azimuth, and string design.

3. Voltage Drop & Cable Sizing IEC Standards

Calculating the exact DC and AC cable thickness to minimize line losses (<2%).

4. Structural Stability Check STAAD.Pro

Wind load calculations and structural weight-bearing capacity verification.

Financial & ROI Metrics
1. Yield Estimation (PR) Performance Ratio

Accurate projections of monthly and annual energy generation (kWh).

2. Tariff & Savings Modeling Net-Metering

Factoring in grid electricity costs, fixed charges, and export tariffs.

3. Payback Period Calculation ROI

Detailed calculation of break-even points, usually between 3-5 years.

4. LCOE Long-Term Cost

Cost per unit of electricity generated over the 25-year lifetime of the plant.
