ABOUT KIANA GREEN

Reliable, Practical, Built on Trust

Kiana Green is a leading solar EPC and technology company dedicated to driving renewable energy adoption across India.

OUR FOCUS

Understanding Requirements First, Then Offering Solutions

Our philosophy is simple: we do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Every roof is different, every client has unique requirements, and every energy consumption profile is distinct.

Our engineering team spends considerable time conducting site audits, analyzing shading profiles, and evaluating electrical loads. We recommend systems only after ensuring they are technically viable and financially beneficial for the client.

OUR ROLE

Part of India's Growing Renewable Energy Ecosystem

We are proud to contribute to the nation’s goal of achieving 500 GW of non-fossil fuel capacity by 2030. By empowering homes and businesses with clean energy, we help reduce dependence on traditional power grids and fossil fuels.

We actively participate in government initiatives and work with state distribution companies (DISCOMs) to simplify net-metering and subsidy processes for our clients.
