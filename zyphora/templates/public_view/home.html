FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers for Smart Solar Decisions

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar subsidies, installation requirements, and return on investment.

Under the central government scheme, residential rooftops receive direct bank transfer (DBT) subsidies: ₹30,000 for a 1 kW system, ₹60,000 for a 2 kW system, and ₹78,000 for 3 kW and above. We manage the national portal registration and all technical documentation on your behalf.

Typically, 1 kW requires approximately 80 to 100 square feet of shadow-free rooftop area. For a standard 3 kW residential setup (which produces ~360 units per month), you need around 250 to 300 square feet of roof space.

Not at all! We use heavy chemical anchor fastenings and rubber waterproofing sealants on RCC roofs. Alternatively, you can choose our Patented Leakproof Solar Roof, which elevates the structure as a shaded waterproof canopy with zero water dripping, protecting your terrace completely.

Modern Mono PERC panels still generate power under diffuse daylight during cloudy weather. On an on-grid system, the grid supplies any shortfall automatically. If you want backup during power grid outages, we integrate hybrid inverters with Lithium battery storage.

With electricity tariffs between ₹7 to ₹9 per unit and the ₹78,000 subsidy, most residential setups recover their complete capital investment within 3.0 to 3.5 years. Commercial and industrial plants typically achieve payback in 2.5 to 3 years.
